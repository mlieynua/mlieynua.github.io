Miyu Oba (大羽未悠) is a third-year PhD student at the Natural Language Processing Laboratory, Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST), supervised by Prof. [Taro Watanabe](https://sites.google.com/site/tarowtnb/). Her recent work focuses on language models (LLM) and multimodal models (VLA,VLM).
